## The data we process

- Account: your email address and your password. The password is stored encrypted by our hosting provider; nobody can read it.
- Learning: the languages you chose, your journals and their corrections, your vocabulary, your exercise answers, your exam simulations and the statistics calculated from this data.
- Purchases: the plan or product bought, the dates and the transaction identifier sent by Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details stay with Google or Apple; we never have access to them.
- Ads, for free accounts: your device's advertising identifier and technical data collected by Google AdMob, according to your consent choices. When you watch a video to earn a reward, your account's internal identifier is sent to Google, which uses it to confirm to us that the video was watched.
- Reports: when you report content produced by artificial intelligence, we keep the report and your optional comment to review that content and improve the service.
- Technical data: measurements of artificial intelligence usage (model used, number of tokens) linked to your account, and server error logs that never contain the text of your journals.

Your draft stays on your device, encrypted. Dictation uses your device's speech recognition service (Google or Apple): we only receive the resulting text, as if you had typed it. Reading aloud uses the device's speech synthesis. The app does not access your location, contacts or photos.

## Why we process it

- To provide the service: account, journal correction, history, vocabulary, exercises, statistics and export. Legal basis: performance of the contract (the terms of use).
- To manage your subscriptions and purchases. Legal basis: performance of the contract.
- To show ads to free accounts, with your consent where the law requires it. You can change your mind at any time in Profile, "Ad privacy".
- To protect the service and control its costs: usage limits, abuse prevention, artificial intelligence cost per user and share of returning users, measured internally only. Legal basis: our legitimate interest.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

## Who it is shared with

We use service providers who process data on our behalf, only for what they need:

- Supabase: database hosted in the European Union, authentication and server functions.
- Anthropic (Claude): journal correction, rephrasing, exercises and exam simulations. Anthropic receives the text to process, the language you are learning and the app's language, never your email address or your account identifier.
- Google AdMob: ads for free accounts.
- RevenueCat: tracking of subscriptions and purchases.
- An email delivery provider: confirmation and password reset codes.
- Google Play and the App Store: payments, under their own privacy rules.

## Transfers outside the European Union

Anthropic, Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States. These transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, or by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework when the provider is certified under it.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and content are kept as long as your account exists. Journals that are no longer visible on the free plan are kept and become visible again with Premium.
- When you delete your account (Profile, "Delete my account"), your account and all your data are erased immediately and permanently. Only artificial intelligence usage measurements are kept, with no link to you.
- Without access to the app, you can request the deletion of your account by writing to [TO COMPLETE: contact email] from your account's email address: it is deleted within 30 days.
- Server error logs are deleted automatically after a limited period (90 days at most).
- Google, Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase data according to their own obligations.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, and you can object to its processing. You can delete your account yourself from the app and withdraw your consent to ads at any time. You can also set instructions about what happens to your data after your death.

Write to us at [TO COMPLETE: contact email]; we reply within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or with the data protection authority of your country.

## Security

Exchanges between the app and the server are encrypted. In the database, each account can only access its own data. Your session is kept in your device's secure storage.

## Minimum age

Journal de Langue is intended for people aged 16 and over.
